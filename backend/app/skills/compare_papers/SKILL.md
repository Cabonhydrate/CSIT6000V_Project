# Skill: comparePapers

## When to use
User requests comparison of two or more papers.

## Procedure
1. Keep the sources separated by paper ID.
2. Compare research problem, method, evaluation evidence and limitations.
3. Mark unsupported dimensions explicitly instead of assuming symmetry.

## Contract
A small matrix or separate mini-sections, with a citation for every paper-specific claim. Never transfer a metric or conclusion between papers.
